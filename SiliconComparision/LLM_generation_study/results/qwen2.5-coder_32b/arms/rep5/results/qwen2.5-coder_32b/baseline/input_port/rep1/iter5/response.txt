The issue in the original code was that `req` was declared as a `wire`, but it was being assigned in a procedural block (`always @(*)`). To fix this, `req` should be declared as a `reg` since it is assigned in a procedural block. However, since `req` is driven by an `assign` statement, it should remain a `wire`. The correct approach is to use a `reg` for the internal logic and assign it to the `wire` output. Here is the corrected version:
