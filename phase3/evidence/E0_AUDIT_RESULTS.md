# E0 retro-audit: structural cleanliness of recorded passes (Phase 2, Sets 1 and 3)

Date 2026-09-24. Laptop, WSL, Yosys 0.33 / Verilator 5.020 / Icarus 12.0 (the versions the Phase 3 fixtures were captured
with). No LLM calls. Numbers below are generated by `phase3/runner/e0_breakdown.py` from
`phase3/results/e0_audit_phase2_all4conditions.jsonl` (local, gitignored).

## What was measured

Sets 1 and 3 count a run as a pass when the testbench passes, and stop at the first such iteration. That criterion never
looks at synthesis or lint results, so a run can pass while its final RTL still has a structural defect. E0 asks how
often that happens in the runs already recorded, in all four conditions: baseline, sem_adv, sem_escalate and
sem_escalate_restart.

- **Scope:** every passing run of Set 1 (small models) and Set 3 (large models), 773 in total. That is 2,116 run records
  (1,062 `metrics.json` for baseline and sem_adv, 1,054 `run_log.json` for the two escalation conditions), which matches
  the documented 1,512 + 612 runs minus the 6 + 2 recorded transient errors. Set 2 (Twin) is not included.
- **Audited file:** the design in the highest-numbered `iter_N/` of the run. Checked for all 773 records: the file is
  `<module>.v`, it sits in the iteration equal to `iterations_to_pass`, and it is never a testbench (773 of 773). The
  original `final_rtl()` would have picked the testbench copy in 278 of 576 baseline/sem_adv/Twin passes; that was fixed
  before any number here was produced.
- **Structurally dirty:** Yosys did not synthesise the file, or it has a Yosys latch or logic loop, or Verilator reports
  `LATCH`, `UNOPTFLAT`, `MULTIDRIVEN` or `WIDTHTRUNC`. This is the same definition as `struct_clean` in `p3_eval.py`.
- **Determinism:** the earlier 383-run audit (baseline and sem_adv only) was re-run inside this one and reproduced
  record-for-record (383 of 383 identical).

Reproduce (repo root, WSL):
```
python3 phase3/runner/audit_existing_runs.py --metrics-glob 'phase2/results/set[13]_run/*/*/*/rep*/*.json' \
    --pass-field pass --condition-field condition --model-field model --module-field module \
    --out phase3/results/e0_audit_phase2_all4conditions.jsonl
python3 phase3/runner/e0_breakdown.py phase3/results/e0_audit_phase2_all4conditions.jsonl
```

## 5. Did the defects ever reach a feedback round?

| condition | dirty passes | of which first-draft | of which after feedback | clean passes | of which first-draft | of which after feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | 15 | 10 | 5 | 180 | 138 | 42 |
| sem_adv | 13 | 7 | 6 | 175 | 136 | 39 |
| sem_escalate | 13 | 8 | 5 | 181 | 142 | 39 |
| sem_escalate_restart | 19 | 10 | 9 | 177 | 136 | 41 |
| all four | 60 | 35 | 25 | 713 | 552 | 161 |

35 of the 60 dirty passes (58%) were first-draft passes: the model passed simulation on iteration 1, so no feedback round
took place and no condition had an opportunity to act on the defect. In the other 25, the run stopped at the first
simulation pass, with the structural defect still present.

## How to read this

- **It is a property of the pass criterion, not of one condition.** Every condition stops at the first testbench pass, so a
  defect the testbench cannot see survives in all four. That is why the rates overlap. The Set 1/3 pass rates, the
  recoverable-band results and the cost results are defined by the testbench and are not changed by this audit.
- **What it does not show.** It does not test whether the interpreter reduces structural defects. The Set 1/3 protocol did
  not score structural cleanliness, and 58% of the dirty passes never reached a feedback round.
- **Why it matters for Phase 3.** About 8% of recorded passes carry a defect that simulation alone does not reveal. That is
  the gap Phase 3's `clean_pass` and `silent_structural_pass` metrics are built to measure, and it gives them a concrete
  reference point on RTLLM (7.8% pooled). Phase 3 measures the same quantity directly on its 11 tasks.
- **Vocabulary coverage (from the taxonomy documents, not verified on these particular runs):** latch and Yosys loops are
  handled by production; Verilator `UNOPTFLAT` loops and the `Multiple edge sensitive events` error (6 runs) by the
  Phase 3 v3 layer; `WIDTHTRUNC` on named operands by production and on expressions by v3. The other Yosys failures
  (SystemVerilog-only inline declarations, non-constant async reset, non-synthesizable event list; 24 runs) have no
  interpreter rule yet, which makes them natural candidates for the defect-class expansion in `reSTITCH.md` Part 11(a).
- **Caveats.** Each design is audited as a single file. Rates are point estimates with wide intervals, and 9 modules carry all
  60 dirty passes, so they say more about which (model, module) pairs are prone to a defect than about any condition.
